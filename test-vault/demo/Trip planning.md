# Trip to Lisbon

Five days in May, two people, one budget. See [[#Budget]] before booking anything.

## Travel

- Flights
  - Outbound Tuesday morning
  - Return Saturday evening
- Airport transfer by metro, red line

## Stay

> [!tip] Neighbourhood
> Alfama or Príncipe Real: walkable, close to the trams.

- [x] Shortlist three apartments
- [ ] Book before the end of the month

## Days

### Tuesday

Arrive, walk along the river to Praça do Comércio.

### Wednesday

- Tram 28 early, before the crowds
- Castelo de São Jorge
- Fado in the evening

### Thursday

Day trip to Sintra: Pena Palace and Quinta da Regaleira.
