# Garden plans

A small vegetable patch this year: four raised beds behind the shed and a few pots on the balcony.

## Vegetables

- Tomatoes, two kinds
- Courgettes
- Lettuce and radishes for early harvests
- Herbs in pots: basil, parsley, chives

## Watering

Water in the morning, before the sun is on the beds. In July, check the pots every day: they dry out much faster than the beds.
